# Series bible — short version (full text: `../BIBLE.md`)

**Porchlight**, season 1, 40 episodes. Maple Hollow: a small lake-and-forest town of animals where it has been summer for decades and nobody notices.

## The secret (never shown before the finale)
Storm on **12 August** stopped the festival game **Lamplight** before the last lantern was released → the round is unfinished → the game keeps the moment instead of the memory → endless summer. Needs four seats (eye=Rocco, key=Riot, footprint=Bash, ear=Pip). Mrs. Brook (grandpa's co-host) kept the last lantern, afraid to let the summer go; she is kind, never a villain. Grandpa (Amos Vale, only in photos and stories) did not trap anyone. Theme: *"You can keep a memory. You can't keep a moment."* Memories are never bad.
- The haunted house = the old festival hall: echoes of a night that happened at the lake (evidence, not cause). Decoys: the house, Preston, the hiker.
- Riot playing alone: pieces are back where they stood on the storm night by morning. Twelve lantern slots, eleven released, the twelfth empty. The die falls from nowhere (it goes to whoever the game still needs).
- Finale (ep 39–40): four take the seats, Mrs. Brook brings the lantern, the town plays along over Friendzy rooms, the lake fills with light, autumn arrives, Pip moves somewhere near, the porch light clicks off.

## Places
Lake (lifeguard tower), park (Preston wants to buy it), forest, main street (Gus's garage, notice board), Riot and Rocco's apartment + stoop (porch light), school, haunted house, Mrs. Brook's porch, town line, festival stage, the city (road trip).

## Season shape
8 arcs of 5; midseason twist at ep 20, finale ep 40; about 1 in 3 episodes breaks from the mystery. Season goals: the festival championship (from halfway), the road trip (arc 6), saving the park, the mystery.

## Rules
- **Clue tiers:** eps 1–19 odd summer only; 20–35 the round holds the summer, needs several players, something is missing; 36–40 everything. Before ep 36 the lantern is never visible.
- **Tone:** laugh with, never at; no sarcasm, cringe or romance; plans escalate but land warm; spooky not creepy; a small emotional moment in most episodes; cold opens grab attention.
- **Running gags:** the hiker ("it's November"), the teacher's summer homework, Rocco's snacks, Bash losing, Lord Puddles.
- **Friendzy:** the app they use; real features and real prompts only; never "download now"; never magic; rooms hold 10 players, no video; Alias and Truth or Dare are one-phone.
- **Easter eggs:** lantern-symbol cipher (notice board, the box, Gus's garage, town sign), the storm date, the painted board matching new town things, Rocco's humming motif. No fan-submission page.
