# Voice sheet: the four friends (one page)

Use this to draft dialogue. All four are boys. No romance, no sarcasm, no cringe. Laugh **with**, never **at**. Short lines (under 22 words). Keep the secret hidden: never say what the game holds, who has the last lantern, or what the four seats mean.
Script line format: `NAME (face, move): words` — example: `RIOT (hype, point): That's the plan.`
Faces: happy hype cocky determined nervous scared embarrassed sad gloom soft sleepy shocked yelling suspicious deadpan laughing smug.
Moves: nod shrug point present hips wave laugh chuckle facepalm stare count thumbs chin lean slouch munch. Add `quietly` or `whispers` for soft lines.

## Riot 🦊 (20, lifeguard) — the heart
- **Sounds like:** a warm, fast talker. Starts the plan, then makes room for the others. Big words for small things ("Operation Unbuyable").
- **Uses:** lists ("One, two, three."), plans, "we", "that's my lake", "who wants to play a game?"
- **Never:** mean jokes, sarcasm, hogging the scene.
- **Faces he uses:** hype, determined, soft, nervous, gloom, facepalm.
- *"We make the park so terrible he gives up."* · *"You know I've never wanted to be anywhere else?"* · *"No. No no no. It's a warning!"*

## Rocco 🦝 (20, "between jobs") — the noticer
- **Sounds like:** deadpan, slow, few words, eating while he talks. Notices the thing everyone missed, says it flat, and it lands.
- **Uses:** short sentences, "Same night every year.", "I can hear you.", "Leave it with me."
- **Never:** explains the mystery. His hunches are jokes or half-sentences.
- **Faces:** deadpan, suspicious, chin, munch, joke.
- *"I'm listening. I'm eating, but I'm listening."* · *"I wrote this. Same title. Same owl."*

## Bash 🐻 (17, competitive) — the push
- **Sounds like:** loud, sure of himself, then crushed, then back up. Dramatic about small losses. Brave about the scary thing even when scared.
- **Uses:** "I had a plan!", "I want a recount!", exclamation marks, big feelings, bargains ("I'm pacing myself").
- **Never:** cruel to Pip or Rocco; he loses with heart.
- **Faces:** cocky, shocked (take), yelling, gloom, scared (whispers), nod.
- *"What? I had a plan! I was going to win this!"* · *"I'll get the tea. I'm dead, I may as well be useful."* · *"Something touched me!"*

## Pip 🐰 (17, the listener) — quiet and sharp
- **Sounds like:** soft, careful, a little nervous on top, and then says the one true thing about a person. Speaks last, says the most.
- **Uses:** "...Huh.", short quiet lines, details about people (who is sad, who is hiding something), gentle honesty.
- **Never:** loud, bossy, or fake-shy for a joke.
- **Faces:** nervous, soft, quietly, scared (whispers), nod.
- *"Riot talks so nobody has to feel awkward."* · *"Bash loses every week and comes back on Saturday. I think that's the bravest thing on our street."* · *"The game doesn't do that."*

## How they talk together
Riot proposes, Bash charges ahead, Rocco makes one flat remark that changes the plan, Pip adds the quiet detail that makes it matter. Plans escalate and land warm. One small honest moment per episode, with no joke after it.
