WALT (happy): Yes, I know. That's why it's one sandwich.

== The crate | walt garage | walt, riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
PIP picks up the crate.
// Dust. Faded bunting. A hand-painted sign: GAME NIGHT BY THE LAKE. And a folded frame of thin bent wood, shaped like a lantern, with no paper on it.
BASH: Whoa. Whoa! This is it! This is the festival!
WALT (soft): Well, look at that. The spare frame. Always kept one, in case the first lantern got wet.
WALT (soft): I haven't seen those in... I don't know how long.
RIOT (quietly): ...
WALT sits on the crate.
WALT (soft): The Lantern Festival. Every summer. The whole town played one big game by the lake, family against family.
WALT (soft): And after every round, a lantern went up over the water. You've never seen anything like it.
PIP: What happened to it?
WALT (soft): Your grandpa hosted the last one, Riot. I was nine.
WALT (soft): Then the storm came, everyone ran, and... we just never did it again.
WALT (shrug): Funny, that. Nobody ever decided to stop.
// Bash and Rocco are staring at the bunting. Riot has been sorting the same box the whole time. He hasn't looked up once, and he isn't surprised.
// Pip sees that Riot isn't surprised.
PIP: ...

== The choice | walt garage | walt, riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT (nervous): Okay! Sell pile. It's just junk, right? Old flags. Somebody'd give you a dollar.
BASH (shocked): Junk? Riot, this is proof! This is the park!
PIP: Big Hal said if it was a festival ground...
RIOT: It's a sign and a stick. It's not proof of anything.
ROCCO (deadpan): Good sign.
RIOT: It's just old stuff, Rocco.
BASH: You love this stuff! You took a porch light off a house!
// A pause. That one landed. Walt looks from one to the other, and picks up the crate himself.
WALT (soft): Then it's yours.
// He puts it in Riot's arms.
RIOT (soft): Dad.
WALT: Whatever it's for. It's yours.
