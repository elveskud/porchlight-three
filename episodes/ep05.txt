== Mafia | walt dining | walt, riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT carries a phone.
RIOT (present): Mafia. Everybody's got their role. Dad, don't show anybody your phone.
WALT (suspicious): That's what the Mafia would say.
RIOT: I'm the narrator, Dad.
WALT (suspicious, point): Convenient.
BASH: We vote out Pip. He's been too quiet.
PIP (nervous): I've always been this quiet.
WALT (point): And what's in that teacup, son?
PIP: Tea.
WALT (determined): Tea, he says. I've raised a son. I know a guilty teacup when I see one.
ROCCO (munch, joke): He's enjoying this more than the potatoes.

--
RIOT: Okay. Night falls. Mafia, open your eyes. Choose someone.
// Eyes closed round the table. Walt, eyes squeezed shut, is whispering to himself.
WALT (suspicious, whispers): It's the raccoon. It's always the quiet raccoon.
ROCCO (deadpan): I can hear you.
RIOT: Dad, you have to stop talking at night.
RIOT: Morning! Bash was taken in the night. Bash, you're out.
BASH (shocked, take): What? I had a plan! I was going to win this!
PIP (quietly): You were the first one out last time too.
BASH (yelling): Who did it? I want names! I want a recount!
WALT (cheer): I love this game! Why do we ever do anything else?
BASH (gloom): I'll get the tea. I'm dead, I may as well be useful.
// Pip stands. "I'll help." Bash, the ghost, sits straight back down and goes on eating.

== The storm | walt dining | walt, riot, rocco, bash, pip
[storm]
[mood: tense]
RIOT carries a phone.
[sfx: boom]
WALT (happy, looks up): There she is. The big summer storm. Twelfth of August, every year.
ROCCO (suspicious): Same night every year.
WALT: Is it? Huh.
// On the wall behind him, the calendar. 12 August. Nobody has circled it.
[fx insert kind=calendar head=AUGUST big=12 at=4 for=2]

// They all close their eyes. The lights go out, for real. Black. Rain.
[sfx: boom]

BASH (scared): Something touched me!
PIP (quietly): That's me. You've got my arm.

// A door bangs somewhere in the dark. A match scrapes. Walt's face, lit by a candle, enormously pleased.
