BROOK stands at the side_l.

// A huge bear in a ranger hat comes slowly up the aisle. He doesn't take the microphone. He doesn't need it.
HAL (soft): I've looked after those trees for thirty years. There's an oak by the water that's older than this town.
HAL (soft): The ducks nest in the reeds on the south side.
HAL (soft): If you've ever been happy in Maple Hollow, you were probably standing on that grass.
HAL (soft): That's all I've got.
