== Cold open | classroom | owl, bash, pip
OWL carries a clipboard.
OWL stands at the front.
OWL (happy): And so, class, as we break for the summer, one small assignment.
BASH sits on the desk.
BASH (shocked): Mr. Hoot. It is summer. It's been summer.
OWL: An essay. My Summer. Two pages.
BASH: But it's summer now.
OWL (happy, joke): Then you'll have lots to write about.
// He means it. He is completely sincere. Behind him, the window: blazing sun.
PIP (nervous, to bash): Don't argue. Last time he added a bibliography.

== The offer | stoop, sunset | riot, rocco, bash, pip
BASH sits on the step.
ROCCO lies back on the beanbag.
BASH (gloom): Two pages about my summer. What do I write? I lost a lot and got chased by a door.
RIOT (hype): You don't write an essay. You make an event. We go door to door. We ask the whole street what summer means.
PIP: It's one essay.
RIOT (hype, present): It's a people's history of the summer, Pip.
ROCCO (looks down): My Summer.
RIOT: What?
ROCCO (suspicious): I wrote this. Same title. Same owl.
RIOT: You were in his class?
ROCCO (sleepy): ...I think so.
// He frowns at a chip as if it might remember.

== Interviews | main street | riot, rocco, bash, pip, gus, dot
RIOT carries a phone.
ROCCO sits on the bench.
BASH stands at the mailbox.
RIOT (present): Gus! Town radio! What does summer mean to you?
GUS (happy): Well now. That depends on your frequency.
[card: Four minutes later.]
GUS (happy): ...and that's why you never trust a medium wave after lunch.
ROCCO is asleep.
// Bash has wandered off and is holding the phone out to a mailbox.
BASH (suspicious): Sir, in your own words. Be honest.
// The mailbox has nothing to say. Bash writes this down.
BASH (nod): Powerful.
DOT enters right.
DOT: He gives that essay every summer.
PIP: Dot, go home.
DOT (hips): Every summer. Same one. I've seen the pile.
PIP: Okay, Dot.
RIOT (to dot): Great stuff. We'll call you.
DOT (deadpan, stare): ...
// Dot looks straight at the camera. Nobody ever listens to Dot. Then she leaves.
DOT exits right.

== Mrs. Brook | brook porch | brook, riot, pip, bash
[mood: soft]
// She stands by her chair (the chair faces away from us).
BROOK carries a cup.
// Wind chimes turn slowly. They look like they're made of old thin paper, gone brown. Iced tea on a small table.
RIOT (hype): Mrs. Brook! Best summer of your life. Go.
BROOK (happy): That's a rude question to ask a turtle. How long have you got?
PIP (quietly): Whatever you'd like to tell us.
// She looks at Pip a moment longer than she needs to. Pip is the only one who took out a pencil.

== The essay | stoop, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
PIP sits on the step.
PIP carries a notebook.
RIOT: Come on, read it. Bash's was eleven words and three were lemonade.
BASH: It had a strong ending.
PIP (nervous): It's not very good.
ROCCO: Read it anyway.
PIP (soft, quietly): My summer. I don't do much. I sit near people.
PIP (soft, quietly): Riot talks so nobody has to feel awkward. Rocco pretends to be asleep and never misses anything.
PIP (soft, quietly): Bash loses every week and comes back on Saturday. I think that's the bravest thing on our street.
PIP (soft, quietly): If you asked what summer means, I'd say it's four people on a step and nobody checking the time.
