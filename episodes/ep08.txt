GUS carries a flashlight.

BASH: Paper cuts?
GUS (determined): Forty-one years I've kept this basement. I've seen things. Hold your folder at arm's length. Never lick your thumb.
GUS (determined): If the shelves start to lean, you don't run. You walk, with purpose, toward the goat.
RIOT: You're the goat.
GUS (nod, joke): That's why it works.

== The system | archive | gus, riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT: Okay, Gus, we need anything from the old festival. Where do you file those?
GUS (happy): Ah, by mood.
PIP: By... mood?
GUS: That shelf is Wistful. Over there is Thursday Feeling. Don't go in Regret, the light's out.
PIP (nervous): I'm going to make a list.
// Pip takes out a pencil. In a montage of small cuts, the piles begin to line up. It's the most relaxed we've ever seen him.
BASH carries a notebook.
BASH (yelling): Minutes of the Parks Committee! Motion to discuss a motion!
RIOT: Bash, you don't have to read them all out.
BASH (yelling): Motion carried!
GUS (happy): Marvellous stuff. That one's filed under Excitement.
PIP (nervous): Gus, this one's a recipe.
GUS: Comfort.
PIP: And this one's a parking ticket?
GUS (soft): Grief.
PIP: Who sorted these?
GUS (joke): Just me. I've had a very full life.
ROCCO sits on the crate.
ROCCO carries a notebook.
ROCCO (deadpan): Weather log.
RIOT: Is that useful?
ROCCO: Don't know. It's the only thing here with pictures of the sun.

BASH (hype): That's it! That's a hundred years of festival! On the park!
PIP: The last one's here. Twelfth of August.
