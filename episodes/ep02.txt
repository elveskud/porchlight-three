== Cold open | lake, day | riot (lifeguard), rocco, bash, pip
ROCCO sits on the sand.
RIOT carries a phone.
BASH (cocky): Dare. Obviously dare. I don't do truth. Truth is for people with nothing to prove.
PIP: You have a lot to prove.
BASH: Exactly.
[game td: Pretend to knock on an invisible door and act out being hopelessly lost. | bash | Excuse me! Is this the way to... anywhere?]
ROCCO (munch): Very lost. Good lost.
BASH (hips): That's it? That's the dare? An invisible door?
ROCCO: Could be a real door.
BASH: Bring me a real door. Bring me the scariest door in Maple Hollow.
// Everyone slowly turns. Far across the water, up on the hill: the old house.
PIP (nervous, looks right): You said it. I just want that written down.
BASH (nervous): Easy.
// His ears go flat. Hold on the ears.
[hold: 3]

== The walk up | hall outside, sunset | riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT enters left.
BASH enters left.
PIP enters left.
ROCCO enters left.
PIP: Why is it even haunted?
RIOT (shrug): Depends who you ask.
BASH (count): A ghost band. They died mid-song. You can hear them tuning up.
PIP: I heard a cursed bake sale.
BASH: How is a bake sale cursed?
PIP (nervous): Nobody came. For a hundred years.
RIOT: What did you hear, Rocco?
ROCCO (munch): It's old.
RIOT: That's it?
ROCCO: Old is pretty scary.
BASH: But what was it, before? Who built it?
// All four stop. Nobody knows. Nobody finds this strange.
PIP: ...Huh.
RIOT (nervous, to bash): Listen, dares are optional. Legally. We could go swimming instead.
BASH (cocky): Are you scared?
RIOT: I'm a lifeguard. I guard life. Yours, specifically.
BASH: I'm going round the front.
RIOT (point): Don't use the front, the back door sticks but the front's got that loose...
// He stops. Three heads turn.
ROCCO (stare): ...
RIOT (embarrassed): Is what I've heard. Legally.
ROCCO (joke): Busy guy. Lots of legal hearing.
[hold: 2]

[mood: tense]
BASH (determined): Friends. If I don't come back, tell my family I won. At anything. Doesn't matter what.
// He sets off. It's three metres. He takes it one tiny step at a time.
BASH walks to the door.
ROCCO (munch): This is the longest three metres of my life.
PIP (quietly): Should someone go with him?
ROCCO: He's got a good pace. Very respectful.

== Warm landing | lake, night | rocco, bash, pip, riot
BASH runs in right.
PIP runs in right.
RIOT runs in right.
ROCCO enters right.
BASH sits on the sand.
PIP sits on the sand.

BASH (nervous): I wasn't scared. I was warming up.
RIOT (laugh): Warming up for what?

ROCCO (joke): You warmed up for a very long time.
// Everyone laughs, out of breath. Riot looks back at the hill, just for a second. Rocco sees him look.
