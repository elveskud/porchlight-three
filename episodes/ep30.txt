== The bench | city street | rocco, bash
[mood: soft]
[fx snow]
[fx breath]
BASH sits on the bench.
ROCCO sits on the bench.
// Bash doesn't look round. Rocco sits at the far end, leaving a whole person of space. A long silence. He takes out the bag of chips and puts it down on the bench between them. Bash doesn't look at it.
ROCCO (quietly): I've got a thing to say and I'm bad at it.
BASH: You're great at it. You've got seven notebooks.
ROCCO (quietly): I'm good at writing it down. It's not the same.
// He looks at the ice. We've never seen him work so hard.
ROCCO (quietly): I didn't tell you, because if I said it, it was real.
ROCCO (quietly): If it's in a notebook, it's just some guy, noticing stuff. It's a funny thing about the weather.
ROCCO (soft): If I say it out loud to you, then the world's wrong.
ROCCO (soft): And I'm the only one who knows. And I'd have to do something.
ROCCO (soft): So I made jokes. I ate chips. I watched you go in first.
// Bash has turned his head a very little.
ROCCO (quietly): You were more scared than anyone I've ever met, and you went first. Every time.
ROCCO (quietly): I'm not brave, Bash. I just notice brave people.
// A pause. Snow.
BASH (quietly): You wrote "Don't mention".
ROCCO (soft): You were coming every Saturday.
ROCCO (soft): You'd lost the night before, and you'd lost every night that week, and you'd come up the steps grinning.
ROCCO (soft): I wrote it because it was the best thing I knew about anybody.
// Bash doesn't move. His big jaw works. He looks down at the bag of chips. It's been sitting there a while. It's got snow on it. His paw comes out, slowly. He takes one. He eats it.
BASH (quietly): These are stale.
ROCCO (soft): It's been a hard day.
// Bash laughs. It comes out cracked, and wet. He shoves Rocco, hard, with one shoulder, and Rocco nearly goes off the end of the bench, and stays where he landed, which happens to be right next to him.
BASH bumps ROCCO.
BASH (soft): No more notebooks. You see something, you say it. Out loud. To my face.
ROCCO (quietly): Okay.
BASH: Even if it's bad.
ROCCO (deadpan): You've got snow on your head.
BASH (happy): Thank you.
[hold: 8]

== The drawer | city archive | riot, pip, ledger, rocco, bash
[mood: soft]
ROCCO enters left.
BASH enters left.
LEDGER carries a crate.
// Green lamps. A long table. Ms. Ledger sets down one flat wooden drawer. She looks up as Bash and Rocco come in, snow-covered and side by side. Pip sees them and his whole face changes. Riot breathes out.
LEDGER (soft): My grandfather's order book. One game, commissioned for the Maple Hollow festival. Hand-painted.
LEDGER (soft): He took a year on it. He called it Lamplight.
RIOT (soft, quietly): Lamplight.
// He says it the way you'd say a person's name. After eight years. He'd never known.
LEDGER: There's a card. He made one for every game. It went out with the board, but he kept the proof.
// She slides it across.
// at=1 for=6] A small stiff card, printed by hand, the corners gone soft. Pip reads it, because he's the one who reads things out.
[fx insert kind=paper head=LAMPLIGHT lines=["A_game_for_four_seats.","Eye,_Key,_Foot,_Ear.","No_one_plays_alone."] at=1 for=6]
PIP (quietly): Lamplight. A game for four seats. Eye, Key, Foot, Ear.
PIP (quietly): Each seat must be filled, each by its own player. Each sees what the others can't.
PIP (quietly): No one may play another's seat.
RIOT (shocked, quietly): No one may...
PIP (soft): No one plays alone.
// It's very quiet in the hall. Riot's sitting down. He doesn't remember doing that. All round the table, each chair, each night, for eight years, carrying the pieces himself. He's breathing as though he's been underwater.
ROCCO (soft): It was never you.
RIOT (soft, quietly): It couldn't work. It was never going to work.
ROCCO (quietly): No.
// He laughs, and his eyes are streaming. Bash has him by the shoulder. Bash doesn't say anything at all. There's one more line. Pip's paw has stopped under it.
PIP (quietly): The die goes to whoever the game still needs.
// Everyone looks at Bash. Bash, slowly, takes the die out of his pocket and looks at it.
BASH (shocked, quietly): It wanted me?

== Writing it down | car | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
RIOT sits on the seat.
ROCCO carries a notebook.
// The wagon, heading home, in the last of the winter light. The rule card's copied out. They're all in their coats. As they come up the valley, the road ahead starts to turn gold at the edges. It's warm through the windscreen.
ROCCO (deadpan): Say it again. All of it. I'm writing it down.
BASH: I thought no more notebooks.
ROCCO: This one's for us. Back in town, it fades. All of this. The cold, the snow.
ROCCO: By tomorrow you won't believe it happened.
BASH (determined): I'll remember snow.
ROCCO (soft): You'll think you dreamed it.
// Bash looks at his paw. He holds it up to the window. Then he leans forward, and takes the pen off Rocco, and in big uneven letters at the top of a clean page he writes: IT WAS REAL. BASH SAW IT.
// The wagon crosses the line. Green comes in at the windows like a tide. All four of them take off their coats without thinking.

== The letter | stoop, sunset | pip, riot
[mood: soft]
PIP sits on the step.
RIOT enters left.
RIOT carries a crate.
// The stoop. Warm, gold, summer. They're carrying things in. Riot goes up with his bag. A folded paper slides out of the coat he's left on the step, an old one, worn along the creases. It must have been in the lining. He doesn't see. Pip does.
RIOT exits right.
PIP carries a letter.
// He picks it up. He isn't going to read it. He sees the crest. He sees: Dear Mr. Vale, we are delighted to offer you a full... Dated a couple of years ago. He stops reading. He folds it along the old lines.
// He looks up at the door. The porch light. He knows what this is, and why it's here. He knows why Riot's paw goes to his ear. In his own notebook, between two pages, there's a dry brown leaf.
// He puts the letter in with it.
PIP (soft, quietly): Say anything about what.
