== The board | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
// Boards. Dust. They're back in their chairs. Nobody's breathing properly. On the table the box stands wide open, and for the first time, we see it. A hand-painted map of Maple Hollow. The lake in the middle. Carved figures. A little symbol at each corner.
// Riot looks at it as if he's never seen it, though he's looked at nothing else for eight years.
RIOT (shocked, quietly): It never finished.
BASH: What?
RIOT (quietly): The last lantern never went up. The round never ended. So the summer...
ROCCO (soft): Never ended either.
BASH (shocked): No. No, it's just a long... it's been a really long...
// He stops. He can hear himself. And this time it doesn't slide off. It stays. Sitting in those chairs, around that board, all four of them can see it.
RIOT (soft, quietly): My dad was nine.
// He does the sum. They all do. Forty years of one summer. A whole town. And it's Riot's grandfather's game.
RIOT (sad, quietly): I thought I was just finishing something for him.
// A small sound. Rocco's put a paw on his shoulder. Rocco has never in his life done that.
ROCCO puts a paw on RIOT's shoulder.
BASH (quietly): So we finish it. Right? That's all we do. We finish it and it's over and it's autumn.
RIOT (quietly): Yeah.
BASH: That's good, isn't it? That's a good thing.
// Nobody answers. Three of them are looking at the board. The camera moves very slowly across to the fourth, and stops.
// Pip hasn't said anything for a long time. He's the only one not looking at the board.
PIP (quietly): When summer ends.
// He says it so quietly nobody hears. His face has no act on it at all.
