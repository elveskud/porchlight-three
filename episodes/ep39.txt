== The storm | festival stage | mayor, gus, walt, riot, rocco, bash, pip, bashmom, owl, hal, preston, marlo
[storm]
[mood: tense]
[fx crowd row_px=[40,470,980] n=18 mood=run run_at=0.6]
[sfx: boom]
// One flash and the whole lake's white. The wind takes the banner. The screaming starts as laughing. Rain comes down like a wall. It's the same storm. We've seen it. The tables go. The whole town turns and starts to run, and it's happening again, all of it, exactly as it did.
BASHMOM (yelling, yell): EVERYBODY INSIDE! RUN, SWEETIES!
MAYOR (scared, yelling): I HEREBY POSTPONE! LET'S FORM A COMMITTEE! INDOORS!
// The four boys stand still, in the middle of it, soaked, the only ones not moving. Riot grabs the edge of the table. They hold the board down with their bodies. He's yelling something nobody can hear.
RIOT (scared, yelling): STAY! PLEASE! EVERYONE, DON'T RUN! DON'T RUN!
// Nobody hears. A small boy runs past. A rope slides off the stage. Walt's standing with it in his paws, not knowing why he picked it up. His mouth's open.
WALT (scared, quietly): I've been here.
// He's nine. For a moment he's nine. And close by, under a torn awning, there's someone not running. He's dripping, and his great tail's soaked and collapsed behind him. He looks ridiculous, and very old, and very young.
