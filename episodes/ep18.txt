# Porchlight 18: Just a Hiker
next: Turns
thumb: WHO IS THIS MAN?
grade: summer

== Cold open | apartment | riot, rocco, bash, pip
BASH stands at the sofa.
BASH carries a clipboard.
BASH (determined, point): Who comes to town out of nowhere? Who's got a backpack big enough for anything?
BASH (determined): Who keeps saying the wrong month, as a code?
RIOT: Okay, I'm going to stop you there. It's not the hiker.
BASH: It's the hiker.
PIP (nervous): It might be good to rule him out.
ROCCO (munch): I'd like to talk to him.
// Everyone looks at Rocco. He has never in his life said he'd like to talk to someone.
RIOT (shocked): You would?
ROCCO (shrug): He seems nice.
[hold: 2]

[intro]


== The trap | town line | riot, rocco, bash, pip, hiker
// The road out of town. A wooden sign: Maple Hollow, Population, and a number somebody keeps repainting. Three boys are hiding behind a bush that's slightly too small. Bash is a good deal larger than it.
BASH (whispers): When he gets to the sign, we move in. Pip, you ask the questions.
PIP (nervous): Why me?
BASH: You're the least threatening. It's your whole thing.
HIKER enters right.
HIKER carries a backpack.
HIKER (happy): Oh, hey! Hi, guys! Love the bush.
// All four stand up. Nobody knows what to do with their paws.
BASH (determined): Sir. We have some questions.
HIKER: Sure! Have some trail mix.
ROCCO (munch): Thanks.
BASH (whispers): Rocco, don't accept evidence!
[hold: 6]

== Interrogation | town line | riot, rocco, bash, pip, hiker
PIP (nervous): Who are you, please?
HIKER (happy): I'm Dave.
// A silence. Bash was not expecting Dave.
BASH (suspicious): What's in the backpack, Dave?
HIKER: Socks, mostly. A stove. You want to see the stove?
BASH: Why do you keep coming here?
HIKER: It's on the trail. Twice a year, end to end. I've been walking it since I was twenty-two.
BASH (suspicious): And what's your connection to the old festival hall?
HIKER: The what?
RIOT (facepalm): Thank you for your time, Dave.
BASH (gloom): He's just a hiker.
HIKER (happy): I've been saying.
// Bash sits down on the sign. His theory has now also gone. It's been a hard week for theories. The hiker is packing up. Rocco hasn't moved.
ROCCO: Dave. What month is it?
// Everyone goes very polite. Riot smiles very hard. Pip smiles very hard. It happens without them knowing it.
HIKER (shrug): December. Well, it was. Almost January now.
RIOT (happy): Sure is!
BASH (happy): So chilly!
ROCCO (deadpan): Do you write it down?
[hold: 6]

== The log | town line | rocco, hiker, riot, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
HIKER carries a notebook.
HIKER: The trail log? Sure. I write down every town. Look.
// He hands it over. A battered book, years of it. Rocco opens it. He doesn't turn the pages quickly. He hasn't done anything this carefully all season.
ROCCO (quietly): Read that for me.
HIKER: Oh, that's Maple Hollow. Let's see. April. Still summer here. Weird.
HIKER: October. Still summer here. Weird. January, three years back. Still summer here. Weird.
HIKER (laugh): That's sort of a running joke. I kept meaning to ask somebody about it.
ROCCO (quietly): Nobody ever said anything?
HIKER (shrug): Everybody's real nice about it. I figured it was a microclimate.
// Rocco looks up. Riot, Bash and Pip are all still smiling very hard, and looking just past the hiker at nothing, as if he's a radio playing in another room.
ROCCO (quietly): Guys?
RIOT (happy): Hm? Sure is!
// Rocco's seen this all his life. He's never watched it from this close. He's the only one awake in the room.
HIKER (soft): You okay, man?
ROCCO (quietly): Can I keep this page?
[hold: 8]

== Breakfast | apartment | riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT sits on the sofa r.
BASH sits on the sofa l.
RIOT (happy): Great day yesterday. Dumb, but great.
BASH: What was his name, again?
PIP: Who?
BASH: The guy. With the socks.
RIOT: Oh, him! What did he say, in the end?
ROCCO (quietly): You don't remember?
RIOT (laugh): Something about the weather. He's a nice guy. Pass the milk?
// Nobody's joking. It's gone. It's just gone, as easily as a dream at breakfast. Bash passes the milk. Pip's writing something else in his book.
// Rocco looks at the three people he likes most in the whole world. He has the torn page in his pocket. He could say it now.
ROCCO (quietly): ...Yeah. The weather.
ROCCO exits right.
[hold: 6]

== The page | stoop, night | rocco
[mood: soft]
ROCCO sits on the step.
ROCCO carries a notebook.
// Night. The porch light on above him. Lord Puddles on the railing. He tucks the hiker's page into his notebook. It's getting thick in there.
ROCCO (soft, quietly): It's not just me, then.
// Lord Puddles blinks. It's not quite comforting. Rocco looks up at the light for a while. Then down the street to the hill.
ROCCO (quietly): Okay.
