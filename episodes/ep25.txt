[mood: soft]

RIOT (soft): Okay. No rules. You can touch it.
BASH (hype): Really?
RIOT (soft): Bash. Please, be careful.
// Bash picks up one little carved figure, in the tips of two paws. He looks as if he's holding an egg.
BASH (soft, whispers): It's got a tiny hat.

== The long game | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: party]
RIOT (determined): Each square's a place in town.
RIOT (determined): The figure goes there, you turn the card, it tells you what happens.
RIOT (determined): The storm night's where it stopped.
PIP: We just keep playing from there?
RIOT (hype): We just keep playing. All the way to the end. I've done it a thousand times, but never with anybody.
// Montage. Rocco moves a piece with one claw and nods. Bash rolls, and the lantern comes up, and it counts for everything. Pip reads a card and laughs out loud. Riot's laughing. He's actually laughing, here, in this room. We've never seen that before.
ROCCO (munch): Square nine. "Help a neighbour." I'm stealing that.
BASH (cheer): Square twenty! I get to go again! Nobody's ever been this good at anything!
PIP: You rolled a lantern. It's always a lantern.
BASH: Because I'm good!
// Bash takes his turn. It's just a game. Four friends playing a game in the dark at three in the morning, and the sun's a long way off. Riot looks around the table and has to look away for a second.
RIOT (soft, quietly): It's better with people.
ROCCO (quietly, to riot): Yeah?
RIOT (soft): I forgot it's supposed to be fun.
[hold: 8]

== The end of the night | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
// Grey light in the boarded windows. Nobody's talking any more. All four are worn out and happy. There's a figure on every corner. They've come so far round the painted lake that only a little of the board is left.
BASH (yawn): How far are we?
RIOT (happy): Further than I ever got. Way further. That's it. We've done it. Tomorrow we finish.
PIP (quietly): Should we leave it like this?
RIOT (happy): Yeah. Leave it. Don't touch a thing. It's right there.
// He stands, and stretches, and looks down at the pieces like someone looking at a sleeping child. He closes the lid very gently.
RIOT (soft): Thanks, you guys.
[hold: 8]

== Morning | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: tense]
[card: The next evening.]
// They come in loud, all talking, Bash with a bag of potatoes. Riot goes to the box. His hand's almost on the lid. He stops. All the good's gone out of his back, and he's already sure. He has been here before.
RIOT (quietly): Don't get excited.
BASH: Why? What is it?
// He lifts the lid. Every piece is back. Every single one, in the same place, exactly where it stood on the night of the storm. As if nobody had ever been here at all.
BASH (shocked): No. But we were...
BASH (shocked): Did somebody move it? Who moved it?
RIOT (sad, quietly): Nobody moved it.
// Rocco looks at the board and at Riot. Pip's looking at Riot too. They're seeing it for the first time. They're seeing this one morning. And Riot is standing there, so tired and not surprised at all.
ROCCO (quietly): That's what happens.
RIOT (sad): Every morning. That's "I come back and...". That's what I couldn't say.
BASH (quietly): Every morning for eight years?
// Bash puts the bag down. He goes round the table, and without saying anything, he hugs him. Riot stands stiff as a board for about a second. Then all the air goes out of him.
BASH hugs RIOT.
[hold: 8]

== What we missed | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
RIOT sits on the edge.
RIOT (sad): I thought it was me. I thought if there were four of us... I don't know what I'm doing wrong.
PIP (quietly): Can I show you something?
PIP carries a phone.
// He takes out his phone. He looks embarrassed. He's been carrying this around for a long time.
PIP: I took this in the archive. I don't know why I didn't say. The festival's game, it's never named. There's just this.
// The small drawing of a lantern.
// He holds the phone next to the box. He tilts the lid up to the flashlight. Inside, burned very small into the wood, near grandpa's initials: the same lantern.
[fx insert kind=paper head=MAKER'S_MARK lines=["(Pip's_photo)"] at=4 for=3]
PIP: It's not a drawing. I looked it up. It's a mark. Someone made this.
ROCCO (suspicious, quietly): A maker.
PIP: If somebody made it, there might be rules. A real set. Not the ones in your head.
// For the first time, something other than tiredness comes into Riot's face.

== Walt knows | walt porch | walt, riot, pip
WALT carries a cup.
RIOT enters left.
PIP enters left.
PIP: Mr. Vale, do you know who made the old festival game?
WALT (soft): Oh, Lord. Some old craftsman.
WALT (soft): Dad took it in for repairs once, must have been a year or two before the storm.
WALT (soft): Took me along.
RIOT: Took you where?
WALT (happy): I remember the train. It was the first time I'd ever seen the city.
// The word lands. Riot doesn't move. His paw goes very slowly up to his ear, and stops, and comes down. All the colour leaves him, and Pip's eyes go straight to it.
RIOT (quietly): The city.
WALT (happy): Lovely big pool they've got. Son? You all right?
RIOT (nervous): Yeah! Great! The city!
PIP (quietly): ...
