== There's only one | stoop, night | rocco, bash
ROCCO enters left.
BASH sits on the step.
// Bash has stopped to tie his shoe. He's been at it a while. Rocco comes out and stands a little way off.
ROCCO: Something weird happen last night?
BASH: Why would you ask that?
ROCCO: You were the only one who looked at his pockets all evening.
BASH (quietly): It's nothing. That die, from the house. It went missing, then it came back.
ROCCO (suspicious): What's it look like?
BASH: Wood. Old. There's a lantern on it, instead of the one.
// Rocco stops chewing.
ROCCO (quietly): Riot had one last night. In his paw. Same thing.
BASH (shrug): So there's two.
ROCCO (shake): It had a chip on one corner, by the three.
// Bash takes it out. He turns it to the light. A chip on one corner, by the three.
BASH (scared): That was in my drawer.
ROCCO (quietly): I know.
BASH (quietly): It's in my drawer. There's only one.
ROCCO (quietly): Yeah.
// They look at it. For a second they're just two people looking at the same thing. Then Bash remembers he's angry.
BASH (determined): Don't tell me you knew about this too.
ROCCO (deadpan): No. This one's new.
// Bash pockets it and goes. He almost nods. Almost.
BASH exits left.
[hold: 8]
