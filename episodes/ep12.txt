== The tape box | gus garage | rocco, gus
ROCCO enters left.
GUS (happy): Ah, there's my antenna man. Listen to this. Listen!
// A soft lo-fi tune fills the garage. Gus closes his eyes. Rocco looks at the ceiling.
GUS (soft): Every week, a new tape. No name, no note. Forty letters a day about this fella.
ROCCO (munch): Huh.
GUS (happy): I've got a theory. Retired sea captain. Lonely. Big hands.
ROCCO (deadpan): Could be.
GUS (soft): You know what I'd say if I met him? I'd say, thank you. That's all.
GUS (soft): My wife used to dance in this garage.
GUS (soft): Hadn't heard a thing I wanted to dance to since. Then that first tape.
// Rocco stops chewing. He looks at the floor. Gus has no idea. He wipes his eye with his sleeve and laughs at himself.
ROCCO (quietly): ...Good tape.
GUS (happy): Isn't it? Two degrees, you say? I'll be darned.
