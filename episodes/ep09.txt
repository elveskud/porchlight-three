== The coach | lake, day | riot (coach), rocco, bash, pip
RIOT carries a clipboard.
BASH (nervous): I need to win something. Saturday. One game. Can you coach me?
// Riot takes a long, slow breath. Cut. Same spot. He's in a tracksuit and a whistle. Nobody saw him change.
RIOT (determined, present): Son. I've waited my whole life for you to say that.
ROCCO (munch): You were just wearing shorts.
RIOT (yelling): Laps! Reflexes! Heart! Let's go!
PIP carries a phone.
PIP (hype): And we're live here at the lake, where a young bear is about to get in the water.
RIOT (determined): Lesson one. Winners never look at the scoreboard.
BASH: Why not?
RIOT: I don't know. It's what you say. Lesson two. Eat something.
ROCCO (munch): I've been ready for lesson two for years.
// Montage. Bash swims. Bash swims the wrong way. Rocco throws popcorn at him for reaction drills.
[sfx: splash]
ROCCO (deadpan): Reaction drill.
BASH (shocked): Ow!
ROCCO: You got it in your mouth.
RIOT (cheer): That's a reaction!
PIP (hype): Unbelievable scenes! The crowd of one raccoon is on its feet!
ROCCO (deadpan): I'm sitting.
PIP (hype): The crowd remains seated!
[hold: 4]
