== Cold open | gym | mayor, preston, marlo, hal, riot, rocco, pip, bash
HAL carries a clipboard.
MAYOR (nervous): After careful review, and a very nice lunch, the committee finds that the park is historic.
BASH (cheer): YES!
MAYOR: However! It says here a historic ground must be in use.
MAYOR: It can't just have been historic. It has to be doing some history.
PRESTON (present): Meaning?
MAYOR: The festival ground must be used again before the end of summer. Or the sale goes ahead.
// Preston's tail opens slowly, all the way.
PRESTON (laugh): Before the end of summer! Oh, take your time, Mr. Mayor! Take all the time you like!
// Everyone laughs. It's a very funny thing to say. It's the end of summer, they'll get it done. Rocco, alone in the room, has stopped chewing.
ROCCO (suspicious, quietly): ...When's that?

== House rules | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT carries a clipboard.
RIOT sits on the edge.
[card: First night.]
RIOT (determined, present): Okay. If you're coming in here, there are rules. I've laminated them.
ROCCO (munch): You laminated them.
RIOT: Rule one. You don't touch the box. Rule two. You don't touch the table.
RIOT: Rule three. No flash. Rule four. If you hear footsteps, stay still, they go away.
BASH (scared): Could we hear more about rule four?
RIOT: Rule five. Bash, you have to breathe.
BASH (nervous): I'm breathing! I'm doing it as a choice!
PIP (nervous): Why are there four chairs?
RIOT: There's always been four chairs.
PIP: They were in a line when we came in on the first night. Now they're in a square.
// Everyone looks at the chairs. The chairs, being chairs, don't look back. Riot looks away first.
RIOT (nervous): Rule six. No questions on the first night.
[hold: 2]

== Night two | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: tense]
[card: Second night.]
RIOT sits on the edge.
BASH carries a notebook.
// Riot sits with his back to them, paw moving over something on the table. Close on a carved piece being set down. We never see where. Three friends on a bench, trying to be quiet. Bash has brought a sandwich and it is the loudest sandwich ever made.
[sfx: crunch]
RIOT (determined): Bash.
BASH (whispers): Sorry. It's got lettuce.
// A floorboard above them. One. Two. Three. Four. Silence.
[sfx: step_wood0]
PIP (whispers): Four.
ROCCO (whispers): It's always four.
BASH (scared, whispers): Rule four. Stay still. Staying still.
// One, two, three, four. It moves across the ceiling, stops, and moves across again.
[sfx: step_wood0]
ROCCO (suspicious, whispers): That's not somebody walking around.
PIP: What is it?
ROCCO (whispers): They stop. Then the next one goes. Then they stop.
ROCCO (quietly): That's turns.
// Riot's paw has stopped moving. He doesn't turn round. Bash very slowly finishes chewing.
BASH (scared, whispers): Who's playing?

== Morning after | lake, day | rocco, bash, pip
ROCCO sits on the sand.
BASH sits on the sand.
PIP sits on the sand.
// The three of them, all exhausted, with sunglasses on. The new lifeguard on the tower is the duck. Somebody has given it a small whistle. Nobody asks.
BASH (sleepy): How has he done this for years? I've done two nights. I can taste time.
PIP (quietly): He didn't let us see his hands.
BASH: What?
PIP: He says he's playing. But every time you moved he covered it with his arm. Like he's embarrassed.
ROCCO (munch): He is.
BASH: Of what? It's a board game.
ROCCO (deadpan): Ask him what it's doing in the morning.
BASH: What does that mean?
ROCCO: Don't know. It's what he nearly said. Every time I think I've got it, I come back and...
PIP (soft): And what?
// None of them can finish it. A small breeze. The duck blows its whistle at nothing.
[hold: 5]

== The rule | hall, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
[card: Third night.]
RIOT sits on the edge.
BASH enters left.
BASH: I've been thinking about your rules.
RIOT (sleepy): Yeah?
BASH (determined): There's one you missed. Rule seven.
// He takes a marker out of his pocket, walks over, and writes on the laminated sheet. It squeaks. Riot watches him do it.
RIOT (soft, looks down): Rule seven. Riot goes home by two.
BASH: You're on a week off. You're going to spend it sleeping. I'll walk you back.
RIOT (soft): That's not how rules work.
BASH (cocky): I'm very scared in here and I'm doing it anyway, so I get a rule.
// A long look. Riot nearly smiles. It's the most tired smile in Maple Hollow. He picks up his flashlight, closes the lid, and stands.
RIOT (soft): Two o'clock.
BASH (nod): Two o'clock.
RIOT exits left.
BASH exits left.
