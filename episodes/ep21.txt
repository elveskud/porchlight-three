== Cold open | apartment | riot, rocco, bash, pip
RIOT stands at the sofa.
RIOT carries a clipboard.
RIOT (determined, present): Okay. Nobody slept. We know what we know. So what do we do?
BASH (hype): Tell everyone! Obviously! It's the biggest news in the history of news!
PIP (quietly): Do you think they'll believe it?
BASH: We saw it! We've got proof! We'll say it plain, in simple words. How hard can it be?
ROCCO (munch, joke): Bash. How much do you remember about Dave?
BASH: Who's Dave?

== Telling Bash's mom | bash home | bash, bashmom
BASH (determined, count): Mom. I need you to sit down. It has been summer for forty years.
BASHMOM (happy, yelling): I KNOW, SWEETIE! ISN'T IT LOVELY?
BASH: No. Mom. The same summer. It never ended. There's been no autumn since before you were born.
BASHMOM (happy, yelling): THAT'S NICE! WHO WANTS POTATO?
BASH (shocked): Do you hear what I'm saying?
// She stops. She looks at him, and for a moment something crosses her face, some tiny lost thing. She blinks. It's gone. Her smile comes back, warm and huge.
BASHMOM (happy): Long summer! You should wear a hat.

== Telling Walt | walt porch | walt, riot
[mood: soft]
WALT carries a cup.
RIOT enters left.
RIOT (soft): Dad. The night of the storm. It's why it's always summer.
RIOT (soft): You're nine in it, and you never got any older, I mean, you did, but the summer...
// It all comes out. We can't quite hear it; the sound drops away. It's all there in his paws and his eyes. He finishes, out of breath. Walt has listened to every word, with his whole face.
WALT (soft): Son.
RIOT (soft): Yeah?
WALT (soft): That's the most I've heard you say in years. I'm glad.
RIOT (soft): But do you...
WALT (happy): It's been a long summer, hasn't it? Nice, though. Have you eaten?
// He means it, and he's gone. It's the kindest wall Riot has ever walked into. He stands there for a moment.
RIOT (sad, quietly): Yeah. I've eaten.

== Committee | main street | riot, rocco, bash, pip, mayor, preston, marlo, hal
MAYOR (hype): Citizens! The committee has reached a decision! To keep the park historic, it must be used.
MAYOR (hype): So, by popular demand, or at any rate by demand...
HAL carries a clipboard.
HAL (happy): We're bringing back the Lantern Festival.
BASH (shocked): The what?
RIOT (shocked): You're doing what?
MAYOR (cheer): The first in forty years! A town game championship, right down by the lake! Families against families! Final night, the festival!
// Riot and Rocco look at each other very slowly. A town that doesn't know what it's doing has just reached up and laid its paw on the exact thing.
PIP (nervous): When? When's the festival?
MAYOR (happy): We've checked the old records. It'll be on the same date as the last one, for tradition.
HAL: Twelfth of August.
ROCCO (deadpan): Storm.
MAYOR (happy): Hm?
ROCCO: There's a storm that night. Every year.
MAYOR: Oh, I'm sure it'll blow over! Let's form a committee!
PRESTON enters right.
MARLO enters right.
PRESTON (present): Wonderful. Marvellous. And I'd be delighted to sponsor it.
RIOT (shocked): You?
PRESTON (happy): If you boys are going to lose, I'd like it to be in very good lighting.
// He tips his hat. He looks a little too long at Riot, and at the house on the hill, and he goes.
[hold: 6]

== Four of us | stoop, night | riot, rocco, bash, pip
[mood: soft]
RIOT sits on the step.
RIOT (soft): The whole town's going to be standing at that lake, on that night, with no idea.
BASH (chin): But we do.
BASH (determined): So we've got until the twelfth to finish that game. Then it's autumn and I can finally see what a leaf does.
// He laughs. Riot laughs. Rocco nearly does. It feels like the first night of something. Pip laughs last, and a beat too late, and Rocco's eyes go to him, just briefly.
PIP (happy): Yeah. Autumn.
