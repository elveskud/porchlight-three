## 1:12 — The last lantern
*memory lake. With: riot, rocco, bash, pip, brook, walt, preston.*

*It goes up. Small and gold into the black rain. It doesn't go out. It rises over the water and the rain stops, all at once, like a held breath let go. Every drop falls and none follow. The clouds roll back from it. There are stars.*

*On the board, the twelfth marker's there, on its peg. Nobody put it there. The figure moves to the last square, and stays.*

*And the lake fills with light. Eleven lanterns' worth, forty years' worth, every summer that was ever kept, rising up from the water all at once, all round the ring of porch lights. All the town's on the shore, with their faces lifted. People are turning to each other. An old bear's crying. They all remember. For a moment everybody remembers.*

`1:13` **WALT** *(soft, quietly)*: That's how it ended. That's how it was supposed to end.

*A wind comes across the water. It's cool. It's the first cool wind in forty years. It moves the fur on Riot's face. High up on the old oak, one yellow leaf lets go, and it turns, and it falls.*
