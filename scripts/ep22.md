## 1:25 — Bash on the case

`1:33` **BASH**: Thursdays. Someone goes to Gus's on Thursdays. Someone who's up late.

`1:38` **BASH**: Someone who knows Gus's garage, and knows how to get in without waking the goat.

`1:44` **ROCCO** *(deadpan)*: Sounds like a bad guy.

`1:47` **BASH**: And another thing. All the tapes have this bit, this hum.

*He hums it. It's right. It's exactly right. He doesn't know he's been humming it all week. He frowns.*

`1:52` **BASH** *(suspicious)*: I've heard somebody doing that. Close by.

*A very long silence. Bash looks round the room. The sofa. The fan. The beanbag. He looks at Rocco, and at the bag of chips. Rocco doesn't blink.*

`1:55` **BASH** *(shrug)*: It'll come to me. You're no use anyway.

*BASH exits right.*

`1:59` **ROCCO** *(quietly)*: ...Yeah.

*He stays where he is, breathing. It wasn't relief on his face. It was something closer to disappointment.*

## 2:42 — Humming

*ROCCO enters left.*
